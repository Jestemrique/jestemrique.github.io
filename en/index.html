Hi, I'm Enrique

This is my personal site, and it works like a notebook: I write down what I learn about pretty much anything — tools I work with, development, the web and also, why not, some personal stuff.

For now I'm mostly into things related to my job: APIs, containers and, lately, AI and agents. Basically I learn by building things and breaking them. This very site is one of them: it's hand-made—with help from Claude Code—it deploys itself, and every piece in it is something I had to understand before putting it in.

The best way I know to learn something is by writing it down, which is why I decided to do that here. So don't expect finished tutorials: in the articles you'll find what I struggled with, what I got wrong the first time, and what I'd do differently now. And if it happens to help someone else too, great.
